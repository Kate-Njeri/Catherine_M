The Challenge

The company already had valuable product, project, and industry assets, but those assets needed to be turned into a more structured and consistent content workflow. The goal was to strengthen Instagram as a channel for reaching a specific professional project audience, particularly in the US and Canada.
The work was not simply about creating more posts. It was about creating a repeatable system for deciding what to publish, why it matters to the target audience, how existing assets can be reused, and how performance can be measured.

My Approach

1

Audit & Baseline

Reviewed the existing Instagram presence, available marketing assets, and audience priorities to establish a starting point for the content system and measurement plan.

2

Audience & Content Structure

Structured the content around the companies' Ideal Client Profile(ICP), with a priority on US and Canadian markets.

3

30-Day Content System

Built a 30-day content calendar with clear pillars, formats, objectives, calls to action, and publishing dates, making the content workflow easier to review and execute.

4

Approval & Publishing Workflow

Created a simple approval process before scheduling and structured the workflow around Meta Business Suite so the company retains ownership of the account and publishing process.

What I Built

  • Instagram profile structure — refined the profile around clearer positioning, discoverability, calls to action, and professional presentation.
  • 30-day content calendar — mapped content ideas to pillars, formats, audience, objectives, CTAs and publishing dates.
  • Content repurposing workflow — identified ways to turn existing product, project and industry assets into multiple content opportunities.
  • Analytics tracking plan — established a baseline for monitoring Instagram performance and connecting content activity with audience reach and inquiries.

Measurement

The measurement framework was designed around the companies' two core goals: building visibility with the right professional audience and turning that visibility into meaningful business opportunities.

  • Instagram performance - reach, impressions, engagement and profile activity.
  • Audience relevance - whether content is reaching the intended professional audience and priority markets.
  • Content performance - which pillars and formats generate stronger engagement.
  • Business signals - profile visits, website activity, and inquiries where measurable.

Lessons Learned

For a product-led business, the challenge is often not a lack of content. It is the lack of a system for turning what the business already knows and already has into consistent, audience-focused communication.
This project reinforced the value of treating content as an operational workflow: define the audience, organize the source material, create repeatable content categories, build an approval process, and measure what happens after publishing.
