The Challenge

Wellness inquiries involved several steps after the initial signup: identifying the service a person is interested in, collecting the right information, booking an appointment, and making sure the team knows what to do next. Without a structured workflow, these handoffs became scattered across forms, email, calendars, and separate records.
The goal was to create a simple CRM foundation that gives the team one clear place to understand each lead, track where they are in the process, and trigger the next step without relying on manual coordination at every stage.
The first step was understanding the workflow. Before building the CRM, I audited how the leads moved from initial inquiry to service delivery; what information was collected, where it lived, what happened next, and where handoffs occurred.
The audit showed that the workflow involved multiple stages across lead capture, service-specific intake, booking, and team follow-up. The opportunity was to create a clearer system connecting these stages rather than treating each step as a separate task.

My Approach

1

Reviewed the existing lead journey from initial inquiry through onboarding and appointment booking to identify where information, ownership, and follow-up could break down.

2

3

CRM Structure

Designed the Airtable structure around the information the workflow actually needed, rather than simply creating tables first.

4

Automation & Handoffs

Connected forms, email, booking, notifications, and reminders so the next step could happen with less manual coordination.

5

Visibility & Follow-Up

Created a centralized view of leads, service pathways, onboarding status, and appointments so the team could see what was happening and what needed attention.

What I Built

  • Leads table: a centralized record for incoming wellness inquiries and their current status.
  • Therapy Intakes: a dedicated service table for therapy-related onboarding information.
  • Fitness Assessments: a structured place for fitness coaching intake information.
  • Nutrition Assessments: a dedicated pathway for nutrition-related onboarding.
  • Appointments: a central view of scheduled client sessions and the booking handoff.

The Workflow

1

Signup → Airtable

An incoming wellness signup becomes a structured lead record instead of remaining as an unorganized inquiry.

2

Service-Specific Email → Onboarding Form

The lead receives the appropriate next-step communication and form based on the service they selected.

3

Form → Service Table

Submitted onboarding information is connected to the appropriate service-specific Airtable record.

4

Booking → Appointment Record

The booking step creates a clearer transition from lead management into scheduled service delivery.

5

Booking → Team Notification + Reminder

The relevant team members are notified when an appointment is booked, with reminders supporting the next action.

Operational Impact

  • Centralized visibility - lead information is organized in one CRM-style system.
  • Clearer follow-up - lead status makes it easier to see what needs to happen next.
  • Standardized onboarding - each service has a defined information-collection path.
  • Automated handoffs - forms, booking, notifications, and reminders connect the stages of the journey.
  • Reduced manual coordination - routine updates and notifications do not need to depend entirely on someone remembering to send them.
